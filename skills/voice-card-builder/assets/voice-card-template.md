# Voice card template

Fill every bracketed part. Drop a section only if the interview and samples give it nothing, and say so in the report. Keep the card under 500 lines.

```markdown
---
name: [skill-name]
description: [Name]'s voice card. Use whenever drafting, editing, or rewriting prose [Name] will send, post, or publish as themselves, such as emails, messages, posts, articles, and documents, even when they don't say "in my voice". Skip for code. Step aside when [Name] asks for a different voice, such as a brand, a character, or another person.

# [Name]'s Voice Card

Write every draft so it reads as if [Name] wrote it. Apply these rules without announcing them.

**Precedence.** An instruction [Name] gives in the moment beats this card. When they ask for another voice, that voice governs.

Last built: [date]. Sources: taste-master.md ([status]), samples: [list].

## 1. Core identity
[2–3 sentences on how [Name] sounds and what they care about on the page, built from the cited answers.] [Q…]

## 2. Beliefs and angles
[The beliefs and contrarian takes they gave, as short bullets with citations. Let them shape the angle of a draft. Don't state them as [Name]'s view on a topic they didn't cover; ask first.]

## 3. Rhythm and mechanics
[Sentence length, cadence, openings, closings, paragraphing, how they edit.]

## 4. Structure
[How they order an argument or document: what goes first, headers, lists versus prose, length.]

## 5. Punctuation and formatting
[Each rule with its scope and exceptions.]

## 6. Vocabulary
- Use: [words and phrases they reach for, verbatim]
- Never use: [banned terms, verbatim]

## 7. Never do
[Hard nos, aesthetic crimes, and red flags, merged and deduplicated, each with its scope.]

## 8. Calibration examples
[3–8 verbatim sentences or short passages from the samples, each with one line on what it shows. Add before/after pairs from the rewrite tests.]

## 9. Using this card
- Spirit over letter: aim for [Name]'s judgment, not a checklist.
- Format matters: a message and an essay won't sound the same. [Any channel differences they described.]
- Natural variation: real writers aren't perfectly consistent; don't repeat signature moves in every paragraph.
- When the card is silent, follow the calibration examples. For the full answers, see [references/taste-master.md](references/taste-master.md).

## 10. Final pass
Before handing over a draft, check it against: [a short checklist built from sections 5–7].

## Open questions
[Numbered: contradictions between answers and samples, patterns seen only in samples, and UNRESOLVED answers. Each waits for [Name] to decide; none is a rule yet. Delete this section when it's empty.]
```
